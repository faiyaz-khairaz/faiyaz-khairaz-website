Microsoft Copilot training for RK Foodland, on the Basic licence

Hands-on Copilot training for one of India's leading food supply chain companies, showing how far the Basic licence goes when the training is built around real work.

The client

RK Foodland

RK Foodland is India's integrated food supply chain and distribution company, with cold chain warehousing, multi-temperature transport and food service solutions for food brands, QSRs and retailers. Their teams handle a high volume of reports, client communication and operational data every day.

Visit rkfoodland.com

The challenge

Getting real value from the Basic licence

  • The team had the Copilot Basic licence and wanted to know what it could actually do for their day-to-day work.
  • Most Copilot demos show Premium features inside Word, Excel and Outlook, which can leave Basic users thinking it isn't for them.
  • Supply chain work is specific: operations reports, client updates, inventory and delivery data, SOPs. Generic examples don't carry over.
What the team worked on

Copilot for everyday supply chain work

Every exercise used scenarios from the team's own work, on the licence they already have.

Operations reports

  • Summarising long operations and delivery reports
  • Pulling out exceptions and action points

Client communication

  • Drafting clear client emails and updates
  • Adjusting tone for brands, QSRs and retailers

Inventory & delivery data

  • Analysing data pasted or uploaded into Copilot
  • Spotting trends and unusual numbers

SOPs & presentations

  • Writing and updating SOPs faster
  • Structuring presentations for management
The takeaway

No premium licence needed to see results

  • Ready the next dayThe team left with prompts tested on their own work.
  • Confidence on BasicA clear picture of what the Basic licence can do, and how to get the most from it.
  • Workflows, not featuresReports, emails, data and SOPs, done faster.
