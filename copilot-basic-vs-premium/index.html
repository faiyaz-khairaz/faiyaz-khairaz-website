FAQ

Questions we hear in every company

Is Copilot Chat really included for our staff?

Yes. Copilot Chat is included with eligible Microsoft 365 business plans at no extra cost. People sign in with their work account, and your IT team can pin it in Teams, Outlook and the Microsoft 365 app so everyone finds it.

Can Basic users use agents and skills?

Yes. Basic users get the Word, Excel and PowerPoint agents and ready-made agents from the Agent Store, and can use agents your company builds on a pay-as-you-go basis. Skills are rolling out to Copilot Chat between October and mid-November 2026.

Is our data safe?

Both licences include enterprise data protection when people sign in with their work account. Copilot only works with content each person already has permission to see, and Microsoft states that prompts and responses are not used to train its foundation models.

Should we buy Premium for everyone?

Usually not. Most teams get strong results on Basic once they are trained. Premium pays off for people who spend their day in email, meetings and reports. The demo session helps you decide who needs which.

We have more than 2,000 users. What do Basic users still get?

Since 15 April 2026, Basic users in companies of this size no longer get Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote. They keep Copilot Chat with the latest models, the Word, Excel and PowerPoint agents, Copilot in Outlook, and enterprise data protection. Training shows them how to get the most from these.

Can the training use our own files and processes?

Yes. Share sample reports or walk us through your processes, and the exercises are built from them. We sign NDAs.

What happens in the free demo session?

A 30-minute online session. We learn about your licence and departments, show Copilot live on work like yours, and suggest what to train and who would gain from Premium. There is no obligation.
