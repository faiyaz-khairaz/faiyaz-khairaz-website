    FAQ

    Questions we hear in every company

    We only have Copilot Basic. Is a workshop worth it?

    Yes. Most teams use only a small part of Copilot Chat. A Basic licence workshop shows them the models, agents, Skills and file features they already have, on their own work, so you get value before spending on new licences.

    Do you run workshops for both Basic and Premium?

    Yes. We run separate workshops for Basic and Premium licences, and mixed batches where some people have Premium and others are on Basic. Each person learns what their own licence can do.

    Is Copilot Chat really included for our staff?

    Yes. Copilot Chat is included with eligible Microsoft 365 business plans at no extra cost. People sign in with their work account, and your IT team can pin it in Teams, Outlook and the Microsoft 365 app so everyone finds it.

    Can Basic users use agents and skills?

    Yes. Basic users get the Word, Excel and PowerPoint agents and ready-made agents from the Agent Store, and can use agents your company builds on a pay-as-you-go basis. Skills are rolling out to Copilot Chat between October and mid-November 2026.

    Is our data safe?

    Both licences include enterprise data protection when people sign in with their work account. Copilot only works with content each person already has permission to see, and Microsoft states that prompts and responses are not used to train its foundation models.

    Should we buy Premium for everyone?

    Usually not. Most teams get strong results on Basic once they are trained. Premium pays off for people who spend their day in email, meetings and reports. The free demo session for HR and L&D managers helps you decide who needs which.

    We have more than 2,000 users. What do Basic users still get?

    Since 15 April 2026, Basic users in companies of this size no longer get Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote. They keep Copilot Chat with the latest models, the Word, Excel and PowerPoint agents, Copilot in Outlook, and enterprise data protection. A Basic licence workshop shows them how to get the most from these.

    Who can join the free demo session?

    The demo session is for HR and L&D managers who are planning Copilot training, with up to 3 people from one company. It is a 30-minute planning session, not a training session. Your teams learn hands-on in the workshops.

    Can the workshop use our own files and processes?

    Yes. Share sample reports or walk us through your processes, and the exercises are built from them. We sign NDAs.
